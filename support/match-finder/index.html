Project support

Help useful connections reach the people who can act on them.

The Match Finder Project searches for publicly visible needs, plans, and capacities that may complement one another. Norman Weiss privately funds the research, protected reports, infrastructure, printing, and postage. Every notice, report, and consent decision is free and non-binding.

01

Only after genuine value

Please consider support only if a Match Finder notice, report, or resulting connection proved genuinely useful to you.

02

Entirely voluntary

There is no requested amount. Support has no effect on access, consent, identity release, priority, or future matches.

03

Funds future searches

Support helps cover research tools, secure infrastructure, report delivery, printing, and postage for future connections.
